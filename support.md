# HonestMile Support

Need help with HonestMile? Email **neosdevshelp@gmail.com** and we'll reply as soon as we can. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. In the app, **Settings › Help & Support › Email support** fills these in for you.

### Common questions

**Why didn't a drive get recorded?**
Automatic trips need location set to **Always** (iPhone Settings › HonestMile › Location) and **Automatic trip detection** on in HonestMile Settings. Very short drives (under 0.5 km) are not saved automatically. You can always tap **Start Drive** on the Trips tab, or add a trip by hand with **+**.

**How do I mark trips as Business or Personal?**
On the Trips tab, swipe a trip right for Business or left for Personal. To do it automatically, use **Settings › Auto-classify** to mark drives during your work hours as Business.

**Does HonestMile drain my battery?**
GPS is switched off while you're parked. HonestMile wakes up when your phone starts moving, so battery use stays low.

**How do I get my tax report?**
Open the **Reports** tab, pick the period and tap **Export PDF** or **Export CSV**. Reports are free on every plan.

**Which mileage rate is used?**
The official rate for your country (IRS for the USA, HMRC for the UK, CRA for Canada), or your own rate from **Settings › Tax**. HonestMile shows an estimate — it is not tax advice.

**How do I move my trips to a new iPhone?**
On the old iPhone: **Settings › Data › Save a backup file** and save it to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone: **Settings › Data › Restore from backup**.

**Can I import my MileIQ trips?**
Yes. Export a CSV from MileIQ, then open **Settings › Data › Import from MileIQ**.

**How do I cancel HonestMile Pro?**
iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › HonestMile › Cancel Subscription. Or in the app: **Settings › HonestMile Pro › Manage subscription**.

**I bought Pro but it isn't showing.**
Open the Pro screen and tap **Restore purchase**, while signed in with the same Apple ID.

**How do I delete my data?**
**Settings › Data › Delete all data**. Your data is stored only on your iPhone, so deleting the app also removes it.
