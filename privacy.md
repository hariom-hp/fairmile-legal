# Privacy Policy

**FairMile – Mileage Tracker** · Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what FairMile ("the app", operated by **Hariom Paswan**, "we", "us") does with your information. In short: there is no account and no FairMile server. Your trips are stored only on your iPhone, and we never see them.

### 1. What the app stores

To keep your mileage log, the app stores on your iPhone in private app storage:

- **Trips:** start and end times, start and end places (names and coordinates), distance, the route driven, how the distance was measured, Business/Personal choice and business purpose.
- **Settings:** country, mileage rate, distance unit, vehicle name, default business purpose, work-hours rule and reminder choice.
- **App state:** whether you finished onboarding, a count of automatic trips this month (for the free plan limit) and whether FairMile Pro is active.

All of this stays on your iPhone using iOS file protection. We cannot see, access or recover it.

### 2. Location

- FairMile uses your iPhone's location to detect drives, measure their distance and draw their route.
- With **Always** permission, drives are recorded automatically even when the app is closed (iOS displays its system location indicator while recording). With **While Using**, you can still record trips with **Start Drive**. With location off, you can still add trips manually or import from CSV.
- Your location is never sent to us or any third-party ad/analytics server. You can change or turn off location access at any time in iPhone **Settings › FairMile › Location**.

### 3. Apple services

FairMile uses Apple system frameworks and no third-party SDKs:

- **Apple Maps (MapKit / CoreLocation geocoding)** draws trip maps, suggests places while you type, converts coordinates into place names and computes driving distances. To provide those features, Apple receives the coordinates or search queries involved directly from your device. Apple's privacy policy applies: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **App Store (StoreKit 2)** handles FairMile Pro subscriptions. Payments and entitlement checks are processed by Apple on your device; we never see your payment details or Apple ID.

### 4. Notifications

If you turn on "Notify me after each drive", FairMile schedules local notifications on your iPhone. No push server or device push token is used. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in iPhone **Settings › Notifications**.

### 5. What leaves your phone

Only what you explicitly choose to share or back up:

- PDF or CSV reports you export, and JSON backup files you save — they go only where you send them.
- A MileIQ CSV file you import is parsed locally on your iPhone and is never uploaded.
- If you enable iOS iCloud Backup on your device, iOS may include FairMile's local data in your encrypted iCloud backup under your own Apple account.

### 6. What we do not do

- No analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
- No tracking across apps or websites (`NSPrivacyTracking = false`).
- No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone.

### 7. Keeping and deleting your data

- Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete it.
- Delete a single trip by opening it and tapping **Delete trip**.
- Delete everything with **Settings › Data › Delete all data**, or by uninstalling the app.
- Because we never receive or store your data on any server, there is nothing stored by us to delete. You can still email us with any privacy question.

### 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, export or delete your personal data. In FairMile you can exercise these rights directly on-device: export with **Settings › Data › Export all trips (CSV)** or **Save a backup file**, and delete as described above.

### 9. Children

FairMile is a tool for tracking work mileage. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### 10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
