# Privacy Policy

**FairMile – Mileage Tracker** · Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what FairMile ("the app", "we") does with your information. In short: there is no account and no FairMile server. Your trips are stored only on your iPhone, and we never see them.

### 1. What the app stores

To keep your mileage log, the app stores on your iPhone:

- **Trips:** start and end times, start and end places (names and coordinates), distance, the route driven, how the distance was measured, Business/Personal choice and business purpose.
- **Settings:** country, mileage rate, distance unit, vehicle name, default business purpose, work-hours rule and reminder choice.
- **App state:** whether you finished onboarding, a count of automatic trips this month (for the free plan limit) and whether FairMile Pro is active.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone. We, the makers of FairMile, cannot see, access or recover it.

### 2. Location

- FairMile uses your iPhone's location to detect drives, measure their distance and draw their route.
- With **Always** permission, drives are recorded even when the app is closed. With **While Using**, you can still record trips with Start Drive.
- Your location is never sent to us. You can change or turn off location access at any time in iPhone **Settings › FairMile › Location**.

### 3. Apple services

FairMile uses Apple services and no other third-party services:

- **Apple Maps (MapKit)** draws trip maps, suggests places while you type, turns coordinates into place names and works out driving distances. To do this, Apple receives the places or search text involved. Apple's privacy policy applies: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **App Store** handles FairMile Pro subscriptions. Payments are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details or Apple ID.

### 4. Notifications

If you turn on "Notify me after each drive", FairMile shows notifications created on your iPhone. No push server is used. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in iPhone **Settings › Notifications**.

### 5. What leaves your phone

Only what you choose to share:

- PDF or CSV reports you export, and backup files you save — they go only where you send them.
- A MileIQ file you import is read on your iPhone and is not uploaded anywhere.
- If you use iCloud Backup, iOS includes FairMile's data in your iCloud backup like other apps. That backup is handled by Apple under your Apple account.

### 6. What we do not do

- No analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
- No tracking across apps or websites.
- No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone.

### 7. Keeping and deleting your data

- Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete it.
- Delete a single trip by opening it and tapping **Delete trip**.
- Delete everything with **Settings › Data › Delete all data**, or by deleting the app.
- Because we never receive your data, there is nothing stored by us to delete. You can still email us with any privacy request.

### 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, export or delete your personal data. In FairMile you can do all of this yourself: export with **Settings › Data › Export all trips (CSV)** or **Save a backup file**, and delete as described above.

### 9. Children

FairMile is a tool for tracking work mileage. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

### 10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
